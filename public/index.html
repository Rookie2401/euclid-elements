TEXT & EDITION

Euclid’s Elements

Greek text of the Elements, based on Johan Ludvig Heiberg’s 1883–88 edition. The transcription derives from the Perseus Digital Library and OpenGreekAndLatin canonical-greekLit project (CTS urn:cts:greekLit:tlg1799.tlg001) as adapted in the WesternCanon corpus.

Heiberg’s edition is public domain. The digital transcription is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The source follows Heiberg’s editorial exclusions, omitting passages he marked for deletion. English book and section headings are editorial aids.

The edition includes 459 diagram placements represented by 450 image files, plus 34 source markers for which the printed edition has no figure.
